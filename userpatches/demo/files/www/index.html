How it works. The camera picture goes from the ISP to the RGA 2D engine, which scales it for HDMI and for the NPU. The NPU detects people with YOLOv8n. The finished screen is converted to NV12 by the RGA again and encoded to H.265 (1920x1080) by the hardware video encoder, then packed into fragmented MP4 without re-encoding. The CPU only draws the boxes and the statistics, which is why its load stays low.

Playback needs a browser that decodes H.265 in Media Source Extensions: current Chrome and Edge (with hardware HEVC decoding), Safari. Firefox does not play it.
